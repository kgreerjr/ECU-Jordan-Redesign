# Changelog

Every tracked version of the ECU Jordan Redesign mod, newest first. Versions 1.0 through 2.4 were private builds. Public releases started with 3.0 on the College Football Modding Community Discord.

## 6.0

- New Greenville Nights uniform, a City Connect-style alternate that replaces Home Option: airbrushed helmet with a night sky across the crown, the Cupola and a live oak on the left side, a storm sky, ECU logo and stadium lights on the right, metallic gold facemask; midnight-purple jersey and pants with a fine star speckle, gold PIRATES and numbers with white outlines, gold | white | gold stripes, skull logo on the hip
- Greenville Nights helmet: "252", Greenville's area code, on the front bumper in the ECU bumper style
- New Home Alternate uniform, with the Away Alternate as its white counterpart: purple jersey with a gold collar and cuffs, white serif ECU on the chest, white numbers with gold outlines, purple-and-white skull and crossbones on the sleeves; satin gold helmet and gold pants matching Away Alternate
- New Away Alternate uniform, replacing Away Option: white jersey with a gold collar and cuffs, serif ECU on the chest, purple-and-white skull and crossbones on the sleeves and a new number font; satin gold helmet with Away's Pirates script, white facemask, a narrow purple | white | purple stripe, the skull on the front bumper and ECU on the back; gold pants with the same narrow stripe and the skull on the hip
- Home Alternate and Away Alternate sleeve skull: crisper edges (no white fringe)
- Every uniform has its own helmet, jersey and pants (no shared pieces)
- Helmet overhaul: gold | white | gold triple stripes on Home and Jolly Roger, gold | purple | gold on Black Pirate; Away's stripe runs the full length of the helmet; a reworked helmet back on every helmet: the US flag and a "No Quarter" pirate flag on top, a CJ2K sticker honoring ECU legend Chris Johnson in his fight against ALS and the number in its jersey's font and colors below (Greenville Nights: one row, No Quarter | number | CJ2K). The No Quarter flag replaces the conference sticker, which disappeared from the helmet after a conference change in dynasty
- Helmet bumpers: ECU on the front, the jersey's wordmark on the back, bumpers match the shell; the red patch on the Black Pirate front bumper is gone
- Wordmarks match: every jersey's chest wordmark matches its helmet's back bumper (PIRATES on Home, Black Pirate, Treasure Island, Jolly Roger and Greenville Nights; EAST CAROLINA on Away and Ghost Ship; ECU on Home Alternate and Away Alternate); Black Pirate and Treasure Island jerseys now say PIRATES
- Captain patches: team captains wear a one-color C on the chest, Michigan style, regular season and playoffs
- Works with another modder's helmet and visor mod (his latest version): visor clips and chin sleeves show the Jumpman for ECU
- Jersey and pants stripes match each helmet's stripe (Home, Jolly Roger, Treasure Island)
- Treasure Island helmet: back number gunmetal with gold outlines
- Treasure Island jersey: no more purple in the conference patch (the AAC star, and the ACC patch in dynasty, now match the gunmetal jersey)
- Names: Greenville Nights, Home Alternate and Away Alternate in the uniform list; Black Pirate Helmet, Jersey and Pants in the gear picker
